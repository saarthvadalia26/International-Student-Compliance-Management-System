# Student Portal User Guide

- **Status**: Production-Ready / Certified
- **Release Version**: v1.0.0-RC1
- **Target Institution**: National Forensic Science University (NFSU)

---

## 1. Authentication Lifecycle

1.  **Request Link**: Navigate to the student login page. Enter your registered email address and click **Send Link**.
2.  **Magic Link email**: Open the email inbox on your device. Click the secure sign-in button or copy the 6-digit OTP code.
3.  **Authentication**: The portal reads the cryptographically secure token, logs you in, and routes you to the Student Dashboard.

---

## 2. Uploading Renewed eFRRO Documents

To renew an expiring eFRRO document:
1.  Click the **eFRRO Status Card** on your dashboard.
2.  Fill in the issue date, expiry date, and document registration number.
3.  Upload the renewed PDF certificate (max 15MB file size).
4.  Click **Submit for Review**.
5.  Wait for staff approval. Reminders will stop automatically during review.
