# Administrator System Manual

- **Status**: Production-Ready / Certified
- **Release Version**: v1.0.0-RC1
- **Target Institution**: National Forensic Science University (NFSU)

---

## 1. Compliance Dashboard Operations

Administrators track international students records:
1.  **Overview cards**: Read active students count, warning alert counts, and pending review counts.
2.  **Student profile verification**:
    *   Click on student rows to open document verification drawers.
    *   Check uploaded passport, visa, and eFRRO documents.
    *   Click **Approve** (marks compliance active and stops notifications) or **Reject** (forces rejection notes).

---

## 2. Settings & Branding Panels

Manage settings via tabs:
*   **General**: Modify support email, phone numbers, and default languages.
*   **Branding**: Upload light/dark logos, custom accents, and favicons.
*   **Retention**: Set compliance document retention periods (eFRRO defaults to 30 days) and trigger manual purges.
*   **Diagnostics**: Click the health redirect link to view system latency and gateway reachability.
